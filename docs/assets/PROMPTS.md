# Landing page assets: generation prompts

All images generated with Google Gemini (image generation) for the Iris landing page.
Cut-out objects are generated on flat chroma green and keyed to transparent PNG; ink drawings are
generated on pure white and placed with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` on the paper background.

## 1. hero-mic.png (hero object, cut into the IRIS title)

A single vintage 1950s chrome broadcast microphone on a short heavy stand, photographed in
black and white, studio product photography, dramatic soft top light, deep blacks, brushed
chrome grille with fine mesh detail, a slightly worn braided cable curling off the base.
Three-quarter view, the whole object in frame with generous margin, portrait orientation, tall.
Background: completely flat pure chroma green (#00FF00), evenly lit, no gradient, no floor, no
shadow on the background, no green reflections on the chrome (the chrome reflects only grey
studio tones). No text, no logos, no people.

## 2. keycaps.png (floating objects, like the dollar bills in the reference)

Three separate vintage mechanical keyboard keycaps floating in the air, spaced far apart in a
row with lots of empty space between them: one keycap printed "Y", one printed "N", one printed
"Esc". Old beige-grey 1980s plastic, slightly yellowed, sculpted top, realistic soft shadows on
the keycaps themselves, each keycap tilted at a different playful angle, photographed in black
and white, high detail, product photography. Background: completely flat pure chroma green
(#00FF00), evenly lit, no gradient, no shadows on the background, no green spill on the
keycaps. Wide landscape image.

## 3. doodles.png (small ink pictograms set inside the manifesto lines)

A sheet of eight small hand-drawn pen-and-ink pictograms arranged in a neat 4 by 2 grid with
large even white space between them, each drawing roughly the same size: (1) a paper envelope,
(2) a woven shopping basket with a small bottle in it, (3) over-ear headphones, (4) an elderly
woman with round glasses and a cardigan, seen from the side, (5) a human eye, (6) a speech
bubble with a sound wave inside, (7) a finger pressing a single keyboard key, (8) a man walking
with a white cane beside a guide dog. Style: loose, confident black ink line drawing with a
little cross-hatching, like editorial newspaper spot illustrations, pure black ink only, no grey
wash, no color. Background: pure white #FFFFFF. No text, no labels, no borders.

## 4. lineup.png (closing row of characters)

A single horizontal row of nine full-body characters standing side by side on one invisible
baseline, evenly spaced, drawn in loose black pen-and-ink with light cross-hatching, editorial
illustration style: an elderly woman with a walking cane, a blind man with a white cane and a
guide dog, a grandfather wearing big headphones, a woman in a wheelchair with a laptop on her
knees, a teenage grandson waving, an older woman reading a letter through glasses, a man with a
hearing aid holding a mug, a small girl holding her grandmother's hand, a nurse. Varied heights
and postures, friendly and dignified, never pitiful. Pure black ink only, no color, no grey
wash. Background: pure white #FFFFFF. Very wide panoramic landscape. No text.

## 5. desk.png (photo for the "one errand" section, like the book photo in the reference)

Black and white documentary photograph: a modest Windows laptop open on an old wooden kitchen
table, a pair of over-ear headphones resting next to it, reading glasses folded on a handwritten
note, a cup of tea, soft window light from the left, shallow depth of field, film grain,
quiet and warm. The laptop screen is softly out of focus. No people, no readable text, no
logos. Landscape 3:2.

## 6. loader.mp4 (loading screen character, Gemini video / Veo)

Hand-drawn black ink line animation, loose pen-and-ink style with light cross-hatching, on a plain
pure white background: a friendly elderly woman with round glasses and a knitted cardigan, wearing
big over-ear headphones, standing, full body, small in the centre of the frame. She listens, gently
nods and smiles, then lifts one hand a little as if saying "yes". Frame-by-frame hand-drawn
animation look with subtle line boil, static camera, no background, no floor line, no text, black
ink only, the last frame matches the first so it loops.

## 7. walker.mp4 (hero figure beside the title, Gemini video / Veo)

Hand-drawn black ink line animation, loose pen-and-ink style with light cross-hatching, on a plain
pure white background: a blind man in a flat cap and long coat walking with a white cane, a guide
dog walking beside him on a harness, side view, a calm walk cycle in place (they do not travel
across the frame), full bodies, small in the centre. Frame-by-frame hand-drawn animation look with
subtle line boil, static camera, no background, no text, black ink only, seamless loop.
